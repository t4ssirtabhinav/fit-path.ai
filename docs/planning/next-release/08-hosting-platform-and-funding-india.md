# Research: Hosting, Platform, Storage & Funding — India-Focused Programs

Covers ticket **#14** — "Hosting, platform, storage — free programs for new companies, India cohorts for full business suite, funding options in India."

---

## 1. Cloud credits & infrastructure programs (the most directly actionable item)
| Program | What it offers | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub** | Up to **$150,000 in Azure credits**, plus access to GitHub, LinkedIn, and other Microsoft tooling, with **no funding-stage or revenue requirement to join** | The lowest-friction entry point of the major cloud programs — worth applying to early regardless of which cloud FitPath ultimately runs on, since the credits can offset adjacent tooling costs even if Supabase remains the primary backend |
| **Google for Startups Accelerator India** | A structured cohort program (mentorship, Google Cloud credits, technical support) specifically for India-based startups | Cohort-based with an application/selection cycle — apply when there's a credible product + traction story to tell, not pre-product |
| **AWS Activate** | Cloud credits and technical support for qualifying startups | Comparable positioning to the Microsoft program; worth comparing credit amounts and qualifying criteria against Founders Hub before choosing where to invest application effort |

**Practical note**: FitPath's actual backend (Supabase) isn't directly one of these clouds' products — but credits from these programs can still offset **adjacent costs** (e.g. a future React Native/mobile CI pipeline, AI API usage if routed through Azure/Google Cloud AI services, object storage for the image-upload features in doc 04, or a self-hosted PowerSync/Wger instance from docs 04/05). **Apply to Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub first** — it has no funding-stage gate, making it the most accessible starting point for a pre-seed solo founder.

## 2. India-specific incubation, accelerator & "full business suite" cohort programs
- **T-Hub** (Hyderabad) — one of India's most prominent startup incubators, offering cohort programs that typically bundle mentorship, investor access, and operational/business support — close to what the ticket means by "full business suite" support.
- **Indian Angel Network (IAN)** — a long-established angel-investor network that, beyond capital, often provides mentorship and operational guidance to portfolio companies — relevant both as a funding source (§3) and as a network/support resource.
- These programs are typically **selective and application-cycle-based** — the practical move is to **identify 2-3 realistic targets given FitPath's current stage** (a working product with early users is a meaningfully stronger application than an idea-stage pitch) and prepare focused applications rather than scattering effort across every program found.

## 3. Government and funding programs
- **Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)** — a central-government program providing **₹50 lakh to ₹1.5 crore in equity-free or convertible-debenture-style funding** to early-stage startups, channeled through approved incubators rather than applied for directly from the government. **This is likely the single most relevant near-term funding avenue for FitPath** — it's specifically designed for exactly this stage (early product, pre-traction-at-scale), and "equity-free" matters enormously for a solo founder who wants to preserve ownership and optionality.
  - **Practical first step**: identify which incubators in FitPath's likely operating region (or sector-relevant incubators, e.g. health-tech-focused ones) are **approved SISFS channel partners**, since the scheme is accessed *through* them, not directly.
- **LetsVenture** — an online platform connecting Indian startups with angel investors and syndicates — a useful channel once there's a fundable story to tell (some traction, a clear ask, a credible plan for the capital).
- **Startup India registration itself** (via the DPIIT — Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) is the **gateway credential** that unlocks eligibility for many of these programs, plus tax benefits and easier compliance in some areas — **register here early**, as it's free and is referenced as a prerequisite or strong-preference signal across multiple of the other programs in this list.

## 4. Recommended sequencing — a realistic order of operations
1. **Register on the Startup India portal (DPIIT recognition)** — free, foundational, unlocks eligibility elsewhere. Do this as soon as the company is incorporated (ties directly to doc 07's incorporation plan — sequence them together).
2. **Apply to Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub** — no funding-stage gate, meaningful Azure credits, low application friction. A good "first win" that offsets real infrastructure costs almost immediately.
3. **Identify SISFS-approved incubators** relevant to FitPath's sector/region and evaluate applying — this is the most aligned funding mechanism for the company's likely current stage, and being equity-free, it's the lowest-cost-to-ownership capital available.
4. **Build toward accelerator applications** (Google for Startups Accelerator India, T-Hub, etc.) once there's a product-and-traction story strong enough to be competitive — these are selective, and a stronger application now is worth more than an early rejection.
5. **Treat angel/syndicate routes (Indian Angel Network, LetsVenture)** as a later-stage step, once the seed-fund/accelerator stages have either provided runway or surfaced a clearer growth story to pitch with.

## 5. A grounding note on funding strategy
None of this should be read as "go raise money now" — it's a map of *what exists* so that if/when FitPath needs external capital or infrastructure support, the founder isn't starting the research from zero under time pressure. The **equity-free options (SISFS, cloud credit programs, DPIIT benefits) are the highest-leverage, lowest-risk early moves** — they extend runway and reduce costs without diluting ownership or adding investor-management overhead, both of which carry real opportunity cost for a solo or small founding team that should be spending its limited time on product and users first.
